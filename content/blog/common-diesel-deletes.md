## The Truth About Diesel Deletes

For diesel truck owners, especially those with Ford Powerstroke, Dodge Cummins, or GMC Duramax engines, the term "delete" is a common part of the performance vocabulary. But what does it actually mean? In short, a diesel delete refers to the process of removing or disabling the factory emissions control systems.

It's crucial to understand that these modifications are not legal for on-road use, but for dedicated track or trail rigs, they can unlock significant gains in performance, reliability, and fuel efficiency.

### Why Consider a Diesel Delete?

Modern diesel trucks are equipped with a complex array of emissions systems designed to reduce pollutants. While effective, these systems can sometimes hinder performance and introduce potential points of failure. The most common systems to be "deleted" are:

*   **Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF):** This filter captures and burns off soot from the exhaust. Over time, it can become clogged, leading to a restrictive "regen" cycle that hurts fuel economy and can, in some cases, lead to costly repairs. A DPF delete removes this restriction, allowing the engine to breathe more freely.
*   **Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR):** The EGR system recirculates a portion of the exhaust gas back into the engine's cylinders to reduce NOx emissions. This process can lead to a buildup of carbon and soot in the intake manifold, robbing the engine of performance and efficiency over time. An EGR delete prevents this buildup and ensures the engine is only breathing clean, cool air.
*   **Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR):** This system uses Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) to convert NOx into harmless nitrogen and water. While effective, the SCR system adds complexity and requires the owner to constantly refill the DEF tank. A delete removes this system, simplifying the truck's operation.

### The "Full Delete" Package

A "full delete" typically refers to the removal of all major emissions components, often bundled with a custom ECU tune to ensure the truck runs correctly without them. A typical full delete package from a provider like **Guerrilla Automotive** includes:

3.  **Custom ECU Tune:** This is the most critical part. The ECU must be reprogrammed to account for the missing sensors and components.
